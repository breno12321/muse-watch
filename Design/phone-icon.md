# iPhone app icon

Final asset: `MusePhone/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon.png`.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then normalized to 1024 × 1024 with `sips`. The PNG is opaque. iOS applies the final app-icon mask.

The final version incorporates the actual blue Muse mark referenced from the installed official app, `/Applications/Muse.app/Contents/Resources/AppIcon.icns` (`CFBundleIconFile = AppIcon`). Its icon was locally converted to PNG for the edit reference. No account data was used. The warm ivory field and small burgundy microphone badge distinguish the Watch companion. The literal white microphone replaced the earlier waveform for clearer recognition at app-icon size. The earlier unbranded voice concept is retained as `Design/phone-icon-voice-concept.png`.
